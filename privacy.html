LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: October 9, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NexCoreMC Network ("NexCoreMC", "we", "our", or "us"). We operate the Minecraft multiplayer network accessible at nexcoremc.in and its associated web services. We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, fair gaming environment for all players.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, connect to our Minecraft servers, participate in our Discord community, or purchase server ranks and perks.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum amount of data required to operate and secure the server:

  • Minecraft Account Information: Your in-game username (IGN), unique Minecraft UUID, skin assets, and in-game statistics (e.g., player level, economy balance, game mode achievements).
  • Connection & Network Logs: IP address, connection timestamps, and protocol handshake data. This data is utilized strictly for DDoS mitigation, firewall routing, and automated anti-cheat heuristic verification.
  • Community & Support Data: Discord username and snowflake ID when opening support tickets or claiming store perks through our Discord server.
  • Store Transaction Records: Order identifiers, purchased rank tier, and activation timestamp. We do not process or store credit card numbers, bank credentials, or sensitive payment details directly on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Authorizing account access and preserving player inventory, land claims, and statistics.
  • Enforcing network rules, maintaining anti-cheat heuristics, and preventing unauthorized exploits.
  • Delivering purchased in-game perks, permissions, and rank titles.
  • Providing customer support and resolving player inquiries through our official Discord ticket system.

4. Data Storage and Security

All server databases and telemetry are hosted within Tier-4 enterprise data centers equipped with strict access controls, firewall isolation, and industry-standard encryption protocols. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

5. Third-Party Integrations

Our platform interacts with select third-party services essential for multiplayer functionality:

  • Mojang / Microsoft Authentication: Used to verify legitimate Minecraft licenses and retrieve player UUIDs.
  • Discord API: Used for community communications, ticket support, and community announcements.
  • Public Status APIs (mcstatus.io / mcsrvstat.us): Used to fetch real-time public player count statistics.

6. Children's Privacy

NexCoreMC is intended for general gaming audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided sensitive personal information on our services, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

7. Contact and Support

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data review or deletion, please open a support ticket on our official Discord server at discord.gg/nexcoremc.
